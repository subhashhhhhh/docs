Fastlytics is an independent Formula 1 analytics platform for telemetry, lap analysis, race strategy, standings, and session replay.

You can use it directly in the browser. Most of the product is available without setup. An account unlocks saved content, discussions, notifications, and other personalized features.

<Info>
  Fastlytics is not affiliated with Formula 1. Data availability depends on the upstream timing and telemetry sources exposed through FastF1 and related providers.
</Info>

## What Fastlytics includes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Telemetry and lap analysis" icon="gauge">
    Speed, throttle, brake, RPM, DRS, gear traces, lap deltas, sector comparisons, and pace distribution.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Race context" icon="flag">
    Strategy, stints, incidents, standings, championship progression, and team pace views.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replay and profiles" icon="play">
    Session replay, driver and team profiles, historical season coverage, and shareable saved charts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Community features" icon="message-circle">
    News, discussions, replies, voting, public profiles, and notifications based on your favorites.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
