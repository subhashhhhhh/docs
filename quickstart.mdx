Use this guide if you want to go from zero to your first useful Fastlytics workflow quickly.

## Before you start

<Info>
  You can browse most of Fastlytics without an account. Create an account when you want saved charts, discussions, notifications, or gated features such as replay and profiles.
</Info>

## Get productive in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and pick a season">
    Start at [fastlytics.app](https://fastlytics.app). Pick the current season first if you are on the Free tier.

    Then open a race weekend from the dashboard or calendar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the race workspace">
    Inside a race page, switch between the main analysis sections:

    - overview
    - strategy
    - lap analysis
    - head-to-head
    - telemetry
    - replay

    Start with two drivers from the same session so the charts and sector comparisons are easy to follow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in for personalized features">
    Create an account when you want to:

    - save charts into folders
    - join discussions and vote on replies
    - receive notifications tied to your favorite drivers or teams
    - unlock gated surfaces such as replay and detailed profiles
  </Step>
</Steps>
