Session replay lets you watch a completed F1 session unfold inside the browser.

Instead of only reading charts after the fact, you can scrub through the timeline and see how position, pace, and incidents developed in sequence.

## What replay includes

- driver positions on the track map
- synced timing and leaderboard updates
- telemetry context such as speed, RPM, gear, and DRS
- session metadata and replay chunks for longer sessions

## Access and limits

<Info>
  Replay access is tiered. Free accounts get a short replay window. Pro unlocks unlimited session replay.
</Info>

## Practical workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open replay from a race page">
    Start from the event you care about, then switch to the replay section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scrub to the phase you care about">
    Use the timeline to move to pit cycles, a safety car, a qualifying run, or the laps around an overtake.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair replay with charts">
    Use replay for sequence and timing, then return to telemetry or lap analysis when you need exact pace or trace detail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to keep in mind

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Availability by season" icon="clock-3">
    Replay depth and fidelity depend on the processed source data available for that session and season.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chunked loading" icon="boxes">
    Longer sessions may load in chunks. This reduces initial load time and makes replay practical for larger datasets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tier enforcement" icon="shield">
    Premium route checks are enforced in the stack, not only in the UI. If a replay stops unexpectedly, check your plan or auth state first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
