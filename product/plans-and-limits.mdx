Fastlytics keeps the current season broadly accessible on the Free tier.

Pro mainly expands history and replay depth rather than putting core analysis behind hard paywalls.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current season analytics | Included | Included |
| Historical season archives | No | Yes |
| Session replay | Limited window | Unlimited |
| Core telemetry and race analysis | Included | Included |
| Account, saved charts, and community features | Account required | Account required |

## What Free is designed for

Free is best if you want to:

- follow the current season
- compare drivers and teams on recent race weekends
- explore strategy, telemetry, and standings before deciding whether you need history

## What Pro is designed for

Pro is best if you want to:

- analyze older seasons
- revisit historic sessions in replay without short limits
- build longer comparison workflows across multiple years

## Notes on limits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Historical coverage" icon="clock-3">
    Historical access depends on the seasons available through the processed source data. Earlier years can have less complete telemetry detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replay access" icon="play">
    Replay is the main premium surface today. If replay depth matters to your workflow, check your plan first.
  </Accordion>

    Some limits are enforced in the worker and backend, not only in the UI.
  </Accordion>
