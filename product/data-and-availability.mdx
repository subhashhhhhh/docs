Fastlytics depends on upstream timing and telemetry data sources. That means coverage quality can vary by session and season.

## Source of truth

Fastlytics uses the FastF1 ecosystem and related processed datasets to power telemetry, timing, standings, and replay features.

## Common reasons data is missing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Upstream source gaps" icon="database-zap">
    Some laps, telemetry channels, or older sessions do not expose the same depth of data as modern race weekends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Processing delay" icon="hourglass">
    Recent sessions may need time to finish processing and caching before every surface is ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tier restrictions" icon="shield">
    If history or replay is unavailable, verify whether the session is outside the current Free-tier scope.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting checklist

1. Refresh the page after the session has fully completed.
2. Confirm the season and session are within your plan's access.
3. Check whether the same gap appears across multiple charts or only one.
4. If a recent session still looks incomplete after a reasonable delay, report it with the season, event, and session name.
