Fastlytics works without an account for much of the core analysis experience, but an account adds personalization and community features.

## What an account unlocks

- saved charts and folders
- public and private visibility controls
- news discussions, replies, and voting
- notifications tied to your favorites or comment activity
- access to protected or gated experiences such as detailed profiles and replay

## Typical account flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    Fastlytics uses Supabase authentication. After sign-in, your session is used for authenticated app requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Set up your username and favorite drivers or teams so the app can personalize content and notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start saving and participating">
    Save useful charts, join discussions, and configure notification preferences from your account settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saved content

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Save charts" icon="bookmark">
    Save charts you want to revisit later. This is useful for comparing sessions across a race weekend or season.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Organize with folders" icon="folder">
    Group saved charts into folders so your analysis stays easy to revisit and share.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Control visibility" icon="lock">
    Choose whether saved items stay private or appear on your public profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
